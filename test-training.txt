NYC TRANSIT DISPATCHER TRAINING MANUAL

Chapter 1: Radio Protocols
- Always use clear, concise language
- Maintain professional tone at all times
- Report incidents within 2 minutes
- Use proper phonetic alphabet for clarity

Chapter 2: Safety Procedures
- Check station safety before dispatching trains
- Coordinate with platform operators
- Emergency protocols: Red button = all-stop
- Report delays to central command

Chapter 3: Common Issues
- Signaling failures: Contact maintenance immediately
- Passenger incidents: Follow incident reporting form
- Track obstructions: Close line section
